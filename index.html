01 / CODEX FIRST

Our main engine.
Our firm favorite.

Codex is the main engine behind Sapiens4, and the one we love most. Bring your authenticated Codex CLI to give your Sapis a powerful place to start.

More choice is in preview: alternative engines let Sapis work with compatible open-weight models and other provider subscriptions. The Kimi adapter is available on its own branch; model support and billing depend on the engine you configure.

Explore model options ↗
02 / REMOTE CONTROL · PREVIEW

Your Sapis.
Within reach.

Control your local Sapiens4 workspace from a phone or another laptop. Pair your browser with your computer, then chat, review work, and manage your Sapis remotely.

Device-to-device, end-to-end encryption protects application content as it passes through a relay. Host your own relay and remote web app for control over that infrastructure. Your computer stays on and does the work.

What is Sapiens4?

Sapiens4 is a local AI coworker app for macOS and Windows. Its agents, called Sapis, collaborate through chat, specialist delegation, notes, tasks, and computer access in a shared CORPORA workspace.

Is Sapiens4 a free, open-source alternative to Grok Bot?

Sapiens4 is a free, source-available alternative to Grok Bot. Its Sustainable Use License permits personal and internal business use within its terms, but is not an OSI-approved open-source license. Model subscriptions, API usage, and relay hosting may cost extra. Sapiens4 is an independent project, not affiliated with Grok Bot or xAI.

Does Sapiens4 work on macOS and Windows?

Yes. Sapiens4 supports macOS 13+ and Windows 10/11, including Windows ARM64 and x64. Both platforms use the same Python runtime and web UI, with native desktop apps and Blindly4 computer access.

Do I have to use Codex?

Codex is our main engine and favorite, and the default in the main release. Alternative engine support is in preview on the kimi-engine branch. Compatible open-weight models and other subscriptions depend on the selected engine and provider configuration; not every model or subscription works automatically.

Can I control Sapis from my phone or another laptop?

Yes, through the remote control preview on the codex/remote-control-mvp branch. Pair a browser with your local computer to use the CORPORA workspace remotely. Your computer must remain running and reachable. The preview supports one paired remote device at a time; it is not a screen-streaming service.

How does encrypted remote control work?

Application content is encrypted between your devices and carried through a relay, rather than a direct peer-to-peer network connection. The relay cannot decrypt that content, but can observe traffic metadata. You can host the relay and remote web app yourself. The web app host remains trusted, and this preview has not been independently security-audited.

What does “local” mean?

The app, conversations, notes, and task execution live on your computer. With the default Codex engine, model requests go to OpenAI services. Other engines follow their provider configuration. Local workspace storage and encrypted remote access do not mean all model processing is offline.

Can Sapis use my computer?

Yes. Blindly4 uses Apple Accessibility on macOS and Microsoft UI Automation on Windows. Give Sapis access only to the accounts and tasks you intend them to operate. The shared computer has one Sapi owner at a time.
