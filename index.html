both ways (•ᴗ•)

What does “local” mean?

The app, conversations, and notes live on your computer. Model requests run through authenticated Codex CLI and OpenAI services; this is not an offline model

Can Sapis use my computer?

Blindly4 enables computer access through Accessibility permission on macOS and UI Automation on Windows. The shared computer has one Sapi owner at a time. Use it with accounts and tasks you intend to give your Sapis access to

What can I use today?

Chat, specialist delegation, individual notes wikis, browser workspaces, task lists, and automated routines are active

Which models can Sapis work with?

Codex is our main engine and the one we love most. Alternative engines are available in preview, with compatible open-weight models and other provider subscriptions depending on your setup

Can I control Sapis from another device?

The remote control preview lets you connect from iOS, Android, or another paired laptop or PC. Application content is end-to-end encrypted between devices through a relay. You can host your own relay and remote web app for more control over your data. Your main computer stays on and does the work; the preview supports one paired remote device at a time
